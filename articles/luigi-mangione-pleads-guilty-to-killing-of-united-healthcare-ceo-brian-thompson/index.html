Luigi Mangione pleads guilty to killing of United Healthcare CEO Brian Thompson

By Evan Chen · August 14, 2026

Key Points

  • Mangione: "I shot Mr. Thompson and he died"

The suspect in the murder of Brian Thompson has pleaded guilty to federal stalking charges in a Manhattan courtroom today.

During the hearing, Mangione delivered a statement in which he said, point‑blank, “I knew what I was doing. I shot Mr. Thompson in Manhattan.”

Mangione expressed no remorse and drew heavy glares from Thompson’s family as he entered the courtroom, according to reporting from the New York Post.

The New York Post also reported that Mangione spoke about the extensive planning that went into the killing.

He posed as an investor at a firm and obtained information about the 2024 UnitedHealthcare news conference through emails sent to UnitedHealthcare leadership.

Afterward, he 3‑D printed a gun, outfitted it with a silencer and magazine, and shot Mr. Thompson in the back of the torso.

He was arrested five days later at a McDonald’s in Pennsylvania, 230 miles (370 kilometers) west of Manhattan.

Mangione’s enmity for UnitedHealthcare has been well documented. He suffered from spondylolisthesis, a painful spinal condition in which a vertebra slips out of alignment. He had a Reddit account named “Mister_Cactus” where he discussed his history of healthcare struggles, including a surgery that, according to Mangione, improved his well‑being immensely. Medical experts disputed that claim, saying the surgery may have been performed incorrectly and could have contributed to his prolonged mental distress.

It's important to note that Mangione was never actually a UnitedHealthcare customer. Investigators say he chose the company simply because it was the largest in the U.S.

His New York murder trial is set for September 8, 2026, and his sentencing is scheduled for December 18, 2026.
