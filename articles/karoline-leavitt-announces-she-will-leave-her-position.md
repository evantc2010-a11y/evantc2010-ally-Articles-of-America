White House Press Secretary Karoline Leavitt has announced that she will be leaving her post at the end of August.

President Trump posted on Truth Social that Leavitt is stepping down to spend more time with her family. His post read:

“OUR WONDERFUL WHITE HOUSE PRESS SECRETARY, AND ONE OF MY MOST TRUSTED AIDES, KAROLINE LEAVITT, WILL BE DEPARTING HER ROLE AT THE END OF THE MONTH SO SHE CAN SPEND MORE TIME WITH HER BEAUTIFUL YOUNG CHILDREN AND FAMILY, A DECISION I TOTALLY UNDERSTAND AND RESPECT! KAROLINE WILL NOW BE ONE OF MY TOP OUTSIDE ADVISORS…”

Leavitt later confirmed her departure in a statement on Instagram, calling it a “bittersweet decision.” She explained that after returning to work, she realized she could not be “the best mom and the press secretary at the same time.”

Leavitt gave birth to a baby girl on May 1, 2026. She returned from maternity leave on July 16 and is now stepping down less than a month later.

At 26, Leavitt is the youngest press secretary in U.S. history.

Before becoming press secretary, she served as a deputy press secretary during Donald Trump’s first term. She later ran for Congress, losing to incumbent Chris Pappas, and eventually became a spokesperson for Trump’s 2024 campaign.
