**Pope Leo XIV has announced that he will undergo surgery in the coming days after doctors discovered a small nodule in his lung during routine medical examinations.**[ \[fox4news.com]](https://www.fox4news.com/news/pope-leo-nodule-lung-surgery-planned-next-few-days),[ \[vaticannews.va]](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/pope/news/2026-10/pope-leo-xiv-to-undergo-surgery-remove-nodule-lung.html)

The 71-year-old pontiff revealed the news during his Sunday Angelus address in St. Peter's Square, telling the faithful that the nodule was detected at a "very early stage" and is well-localized. According to the pope, doctors recommended surgery to remove the lesion, and he has decided to follow their advice.[ \[fox4news.com]](https://www.fox4news.com/news/pope-leo-nodule-lung-surgery-planned-next-few-days),[ \[fox32chicago.com]](https://www.fox32chicago.com/news/pope-leo-lung-surgery),[ \[vaticannews.va]](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/pope/news/2026-10/pope-leo-xiv-to-undergo-surgery-remove-nodule-lung.html)

Vatican officials said the procedure is expected to take place in the coming days at Rome's Gemelli Hospital, which has traditionally provided medical care for popes. Vatican spokesman Matteo Bruni said the pope's recovery is expected to take approximately one week.[ \[fox4news.com]](https://www.fox4news.com/news/pope-leo-nodule-lung-surgery-planned-next-few-days),[ \[vaticannews.va]](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/pope/news/2026-10/pope-leo-xiv-to-undergo-surgery-remove-nodule-lung.html)

The Vatican has not disclosed whether the nodule is cancerous, and few details about the diagnosis have been released. Medical experts note that lung nodules can be either benign or malignant, though early detection generally improves treatment outcomes.[ \[fox4news.com]](https://www.fox4news.com/news/pope-leo-nodule-lung-surgery-planned-next-few-days),[ \[usnews.com]](https://www.usnews.com/news/world/articles/2026-10-11/pope-leo-xiv-says-he-will-undergo-surgery-in-the-coming-days-to-remove-a-nodule-on-his-lung)

In a statement following the pope's announcement, Giorgia Meloni expressed support for the Holy Father and wished him a speedy recovery.

"I wish to express my personal closeness and that of the Italian Government to the Holy Father for the trial he is called upon to face in the coming days," Meloni said.

She added that the pope's calm and reassuring remarks had brought comfort to many Catholics concerned about his health.

The announcement marks the first major health challenge of Pope Leo XIV's pontificate. The American-born pope has previously been known for maintaining an active lifestyle, including regular tennis matches and swimming.[ \[fox4news.com]](https://www.fox4news.com/news/pope-leo-nodule-lung-surgery-planned-next-few-days),[ \[fox32chicago.com]](https://www.fox32chicago.com/news/pope-leo-lung-surgery)

The surgery comes ahead of a planned trip to South America, where the pope is scheduled to visit Peru, Uruguay, and Argentina later this year. Vatican officials have not announced any changes to the itinerary, though it remains unclear whether the procedure and recovery period will affect the trip.[ \[fox4news.com]](https://www.fox4news.com/news/pope-leo-nodule-lung-surgery-planned-next-few-days),[ \[cbsnews.com]](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/pope-leo-surgery-nodular-lesion-lung-vatican/)

For now, Catholics around the world are awaiting further updates from the Vatican as the pope prepares for surgery and recovery.
