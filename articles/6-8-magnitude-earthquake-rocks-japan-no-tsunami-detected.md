A 6.8 earthquake has shaken Japan. The earthquake shook Japan’s southern main island of Kyushu late Tuesday afternoon. 

A dozen fires have broken out and the Japanese media NHK is reporting 50 injured, per FOX Weather. A Tsunami warning was issued, however none have been detected.

As for damages, a shopping mall has collapsed and officials fear that people could be trapped inside.  The Japanese power company Kyushu is also saying that nearly 46,000 homes in the Kumamoto Prefecture have lost power. 

	The Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi has posted on **𝕏** instructions in response to this earthquake including prioritizing human life above all and providing information to the public in an appropriate manner. 

	Japan sits on the ring of fire. The country is no stranger to earthquakes and tsunamis. The most deadly one was in 2011 which claimed nearly 20,000 lives and injured another 6,200. The city of Kummato was also hit by a major earthquake in 2016 causing 278 deaths.
