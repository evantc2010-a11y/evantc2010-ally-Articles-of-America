The FBI has arrested a 35‑year‑old woman named Jessica Bowie and charged her with attempting to commit an ISIS‑inspired attack in New York, according to federal officials.

Bowie pledged allegiance to ISIS and planned to deploy an explosive device hidden in a DoorDash bag before fleeing to Syria, according to reports from the DOJ.

Before her arrest, Bowie had been photographed and observed making several visits to the New York State Capitol Building.

She also spoke with FBI informants from July 16 through August 17.

When questioned about her motives, these were her verbatim words:

“I want to destroy as much of the building as possible and kill the senators while they are meeting. I want them to lose a lot of important documents as well,” and, “I want it to have an effect on the American system. And destroy some of the taghut.” These statements were included in a DOJ report.

These were not her first anti‑American remarks. The DOJ says she also once wrote online, “Praise be to Allah for September 11,” along with another message saying, “When I can migrate, I will poison these infidels.”

On August 19, Bowie met with an FBI informant and was given an inert explosive device and an inert handgun. She then left the meeting and was apprehended by law enforcement.

In a statement, New York Governor Kathy Hochul said:“Today, the U.S. Department of Justice and FBI announced the arrest of an individual who allegedly plotted an attack on the New York State Capitol. I’m grateful to the FBI for leading this investigation and to the New York State Police for their work to apprehend this dangerous individual. Earlier this year, amid a rise in political violence and threats against public officials, we took steps to strengthen security at the State Capitol and across state government. While there is no immediate threat at this time, we will continue working closely with our law enforcement partners to protect New Yorkers and keep our communities safe.”

Governor Hochul is expected to hold a press conference soon.
