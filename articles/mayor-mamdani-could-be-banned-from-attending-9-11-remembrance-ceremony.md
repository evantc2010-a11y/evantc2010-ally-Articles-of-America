Relatives of 9/11 victims have signed a petition urging New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani not to attend the annual September 11 remembrance ceremony.

The petition was started by widower Giovanni Galante, who lost his wife, Grace Galante, in the North Tower. It asks the National September 11 Memorial and Museum to bar Mayor Mamdani from attending this year’s ceremony.

According to *USA Today*, Galante says Mamdani’s presence would be “a disrespect to our family members because of the people he supports.”

The petition has gained more than 65,000 signatures, including many from relatives of 9/11 victims.

Mamdani, however, says he will attend the ceremony regardless.

“I will proudly honor the families, the survivors, the first responders forever impacted by that horrific terror attack by standing alongside them at this year's 9/11 commemoration,” he said in a statement to *CBS New York*.

Mamdani has faced backlash for supporting and taking photos with controversial figures. In 2025, he publicized a meeting with Brooklyn Imam Siraj Wahhaj, who was named an unindicted co‑conspirator in the 1993 World Trade Center bombing, which killed six people.

This year’s situation marks one of the rare instances in which the 9/11 ceremony has taken on a slightly partisan tone. Historically, figures from across the political spectrum—including presidential candidates—have attended the event without controversy.
