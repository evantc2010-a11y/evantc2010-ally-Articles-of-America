Mitch McConnell out of Rehab, new statement released

By Evan Chen · August 06, 2026

Key Points

  • he will continue to work at home

Senator Mitch McConnell Released from Rehabilitation Center, Recovering at Home

Senator Mitch McConnell has been released from a rehabilitation center, and his team says he is now recovering at home. The 84‑year‑old senator spent nearly two months in a hospital and then in rehab. His medical issues began after he suffered a fall in June. Although he did not break any bones, he was briefly unconscious and later developed a mild case of pneumonia while receiving care.

There has been widespread speculation online about McConnell’s health, with many people demanding that his team release a statement or photo. Kentucky Governor Andy Beshear also called for more transparency, sending a letter to McConnell’s office.

“I am addressing this letter to Senator McConnell under the assumption that he is capable of reading and responding verbally, and not incapacitated by means of consciousness or cognition. If the Senator is unable to respond for either of those or other conditions, consider this letter a demand for his Senate staff to be honest and transparent with the people of Kentucky,” Beshear wrote in a letter obtained by CNN.

In the newly released statement, McConnell said, “I was discharged from the rehabilitation center to continue my recovery at home. Elaine and I are grateful for the many well wishes and support from friends, colleagues, and Kentuckians, and for the attentive care I’ve received from excellent doctors, nurses, therapists, and hospital staff.” He also noted that he would continue engaging with his colleagues on Senate business.

McConnell has missed 51 Senate votes and is expected to miss the vote to confirm Todd Blanche as U.S. Attorney General. He is currently the third‑oldest U.S. Senator still in office.
